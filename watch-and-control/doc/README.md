# Watch and Control

See the print and drive the printer from any browser: desktop, tablet, or phone.

## What it installs

| Plugin | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Remote Screen | Mirrors the printer's touchscreen to your browser and sends your taps back |
| Camera HW Accel | Encodes the video on the printer's video chip, so streaming barely touches the CPU |
| Built-in Camera | Shows the printer's built-in camera as a tile in Fluidd and Mainsail |

All three install together with a single service restart.

## Who it is for

You want to check on a print and press buttons on the printer without walking over to it. The camera
shows you the part; the screen mirror gives you the printer's own controls.

## After installing

The camera tile and the screen tile are both in Fluidd and Mainsail. The screen is also on its own
page at `http://<printer-ip>/screen/`, which installs as an app on a phone or tablet.

If you also have a USB camera, or you want a video of the finished print, install "Watch and Control
Plus" instead.
